The Hero City

SVG / PROCEDURAL CITY

Not an illustration. A city placed by rules, then projected.

The skyline at the top of this page is computed, not drawn: every building starts as a 3D box (x, y, z), a 15° isometric projection turns it into flat-shaded SVG faces, and placement rules keep every footprint grounded — nothing floats, nothing overlaps. Then the behaviours layer on: buildings on springs, garlands on verlet ropes you can drag, and four birds whose wings each keep their own tempo.

ENGINE: 15° isometric projection · screen = (730 + (x−y)·cos15°·45, 252 + (x+y)·sin15°·45 − z·45) · placement rules as law

ABOUT

How I work

My discipline is procedural design: decompose a process until reproducibility holds, then systematise it. The goal is always the same — tools that don’t tire the people using them.

MY MOTTOBuild tools that feel good to use. Beautiful colour, controls you never have to think about, motion that feels right — and above all, tools that don’t tire the people using them.

The system is the tool

Design systems are usually conventions — promises that leak. I turn the design system itself, wholesale, into an executable tool: parameters guarantee the identical result every time.

That reproducibility is the floor. The human keeps the ceiling — every parameter stays open to override. I never reduce the human’s freedom to raise the system’s reliability.

Directing AI, keeping judgment

I direct AI agents (Claude, GPT, Gemini) as implementation resources under my own requirements and design. I delegate implementation, then read and judge every output before it ships — my rule is to never ship output I can’t explain.

AI accelerates my hands, not my judgment. It’s how one person, one phone, and a train commute produced every engine on this page.
